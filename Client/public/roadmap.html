Roadmap

What is brewing next.

A lightweight public roadmap for the donation-supported beta. Keep this page honest and update it whenever priorities change.

Now

  • Safety polish Tune report review, content filters, IP bans, block flows, and upload moderation.
  • Launch readiness Finish staging, provider setup, backups, real support inbox, and legal review.
  • Product clarity Make Empty Chair and the AI Barista easier to discover from the first session.

Next

  • Community loops Invite links, starter rooms, and a friend prompt that makes the cafe feel alive sooner.
  • Calm discovery Mood-based room suggestions and smaller default room lists instead of noisy feeds.
  • Supporter gratitude Optional supporter wall or badge, with no paid feature lock.

Later

  • Offline queue Draft and retry messages when the connection drops.
  • Public community Discord or another home for feedback, bug reports, and early users.
  • Deeper Barista Room recommendations, conversation prompts, and reflective summaries that stay privacy-aware.
