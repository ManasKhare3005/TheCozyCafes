Support

Need a hand?

Use the fastest path for the type of help you need. For launch, replace temporary channels with a dedicated support inbox.

Safety and abuse

Use in-app report actions for messages, rooms, and users. Reports go to the moderation queue and include the context needed to review them.

Account or privacy help

Before public launch, set up a real support address and update the legal pages. Recommended inbox: support@thecozycafes.com.

Support the project

The Cozy Cafes is donation supported while it grows. Donations help cover hosting, moderation, storage, and AI costs.
