DMCA Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

This working draft describes the copyright takedown process for Chat Room Cafe. Have counsel review and replace placeholder contact details before launch.

Submitting a Notice

Send copyright notices to dmca@example.com with your name, contact information, a description of the copyrighted work, the allegedly infringing content URL or identifier, a good-faith statement, an accuracy statement under penalty of perjury, and your physical or electronic signature.

Response Process

We will review complete notices, remove or restrict allegedly infringing content where appropriate, notify the affected user when practical, and track repeat-infringer issues.

We aim to acknowledge complete notices within three business days. Urgent safety or court-order matters may be escalated faster.

Counter-Notices

If your content was removed by mistake, you may send a counter-notice with your contact information, the removed content identifier, a good-faith statement under penalty of perjury, consent to jurisdiction where required, and your signature.

Repeat Infringers

Accounts that repeatedly upload or share infringing content may be restricted or terminated.

Contact

DMCA contact: dmca@example.com
