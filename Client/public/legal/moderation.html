Moderation Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

This working draft defines baseline safety rules for Chat Room Cafe. Have counsel and safety reviewers approve it before production launch.

Not Allowed

Do not post or upload harassment, hate or extremist content, threats, non-consensual sexual content, child sexual abuse material, self-harm encouragement, spam, phishing, malware, illegal goods or services, impersonation, doxxing, or content you do not have rights to share.

Enforcement

We may remove content, warn users, restrict features, block uploads, lock rooms, ban accounts, preserve evidence, or report severe abuse to appropriate authorities where legally required.

Reports

Users can report messages, direct-message users, and block users in the app. Reports should include enough context for review.

Appeals

Send appeals to support@example.com with your account email, the action you are appealing, and why you believe the action was incorrect.

Emergency Issues

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. Chat Room Cafe is not an emergency service.
