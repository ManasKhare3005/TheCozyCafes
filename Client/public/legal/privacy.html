Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 27, 2026

This working draft explains the core data practices for Chat Room Cafe. Have counsel review it before production launch.

Information We Collect

We collect account information, profile details, room memberships, messages, direct messages, uploads, reports, blocks, notifications, and usage-related metadata needed to run the service.

Third-Party Processors

We may use service providers for hosting, database storage, email delivery, media uploads, GIFs, AI features, CAPTCHA, logging, error tracking, and analytics. Current planned processors include Cloudinary, GIPHY, Groq, hCaptcha, Resend, Sentry, the production hosting provider, and the managed database provider.

Storage and Cookies

We use browser local storage and session storage for sign-in state, preferences, safety notices, and interface state. See the Storage and Cookie Notice.

Age Requirement

Chat Room Cafe is not for children under 13. We ask for birth date at signup only to verify eligibility and do not store the birth date.

Your Choices

You can export your account data or delete your account from your profile. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of certain processing. Some safety and legal records may be retained where necessary.

Moderation and Safety

Reports, blocks, enforcement actions, and limited account identifiers may be retained to protect users, investigate abuse, comply with legal obligations, and defend the service. See the Moderation Policy.
