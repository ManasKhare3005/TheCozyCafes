Storage and Cookie Notice

Effective date: June 10, 2026

This working draft explains how Chat Room Cafe uses browser storage. Have counsel review it before production launch, especially if analytics or advertising are added.

Essential Storage

We use session storage for sign-in state and local storage for interface preferences, safety notices, and features such as sticky-note positions. This storage is necessary for core app behavior.

Third-Party Storage

hCaptcha, analytics, error tracking, or embedded third-party services may use cookies or similar storage when enabled. Production processors should be listed in the Privacy Policy before launch.

Your Choices

You can clear browser storage in your browser settings. Clearing storage may sign you out or reset preferences.
