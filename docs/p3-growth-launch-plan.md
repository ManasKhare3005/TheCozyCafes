# P3 Growth Launch Plan

## Goal

Launch The Cozy Cafes as a donation-supported beta with a clear wedge: calm, small-room social chat led by Empty Chair and supported by the AI Barista.

## Launch assets now in repo

- Public launch page: `/launch.html`
- Support page: `/support.html`
- Roadmap page: `/roadmap.html`
- Changelog page: `/changelog.html`
- Beta status page: `/status.html`
- SEO basics: title, description, Open Graph, Twitter card, canonical URL, favicon, robots.txt, sitemap.xml
- Ko-fi donation CTA in the app footer and user menu

## Pre-launch checklist

- Replace `thecozycafes.com` if the final domain changes.
- Replace legal placeholder contacts in `Client/public/legal/`.
- Create `support@thecozycafes.com` or another dedicated support inbox.
- Connect a hosted status page and link `/status.html` to it.
- Create a short demo video or GIF showing Empty Chair, the Barista, and a cozy room.
- Set up Search Console after the domain is live.
- Confirm PostHog funnels for signup, first message, Empty Chair start, Empty Chair completion, room create, and donation click.

## Launch copy

Short pitch:

The Cozy Cafes is a calmer alternative to noisy group chat: small rooms, softer conversations, Empty Chair stranger chats, and an AI Barista when you need company.

One-liner:

Small rooms, softer conversations, and an Empty Chair when you just want someone to talk to.

Tagline options:

- Conversation without the chaos.
- Pull up a chair when the internet feels too loud.
- A cozy chat space for low-pressure social energy.

## Launch channels

Start with low-cost channels:

- Personal X/LinkedIn post.
- Indie hacker communities.
- Student and remote-work communities where self-promotion is allowed.
- Discord communities with maker/showcase channels.
- Product Hunt when the app has a stable hosted demo and a short launch video.

Avoid:

- Paid ads before retention is proven.
- Broad "chat app for everyone" messaging.
- Launching to large communities without moderation coverage.

## Product Hunt preparation

- Product name: The Cozy Cafes
- Category: social, productivity, community, AI companion depending on final emphasis.
- Hook: A calmer chat app for small rooms and low-pressure conversations.
- Hero media: short demo video or GIF.
- First comment: founder story, why noisy chat feels exhausting, and why Empty Chair exists.
- CTA: Try the beta and support on Ko-fi if it feels useful.

## Community plan

Create one official feedback home before public launch:

- Option A: Discord community for early users.
- Option B: GitHub Discussions if the project is open source.
- Option C: Canny or a simple public roadmap tool.

Recommendation: start with Discord only if you can moderate it. Otherwise, keep feedback to a support inbox and the public roadmap page until usage grows.
